# Policy Preconditions Escalate on Gate Block

When a **Policy Engine** precondition blocks an autonomous maintenance or session-termination action—via the **Load Gate**, **Lock-Wait Gate**, or **Pooler Cap**—the engine returns an `escalate` **Verdict** rather than a silent `deny`, alerting the human DBA and deferring to the **Advisory Copilot**. Even when `XID_WRAPAROUND` approaches an emergency threshold under heavy foreground load, the **Policy Engine** does not silently bypass the **Load Gate**; instead, it escalates so the **Advisory Copilot** can advise the human DBA on whether to pause traffic or force `VACUUM FREEZE`. While silent `deny` would reduce alert noise and an emergency bypass would automate wraparound recovery, escalating preserves explicit human visibility whenever production load collides with maintenance safety.
